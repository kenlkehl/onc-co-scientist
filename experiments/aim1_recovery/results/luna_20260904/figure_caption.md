**Figure 2. Deterministic recovery of planted hypotheses with named versus masked variables.** (A) Five synthetic clinical cohorts, with 20 independent starting sessions per cohort and naming condition (100 runs per condition; maximum 25 research iterations). (B) One synthetic DepMap cohort, with 25 starting sessions per condition (maximum 10 iterations). All runs used GPT-5.6-Luna with medium reasoning and the Work tool's advertised priority/Fast tier. Agents submitted machine-readable hypotheses during research. Primary recovery required the correct outcome, exposure, contrast, effect direction, and complete subgroup structure, with subgroup precision and recall both at least 90%. The adjacent bars show a 95% membership sensitivity analysis and strict rule recovery, which requires equivalent boundaries. All recovery definitions also required a linked discovery analysis and independently recomputed statistical support on a paired 20% held-out split, with prespecified alpha allocation across distinct submitted claims. Labels show recovered runs/total runs. No LLM was used for recovery scoring. These are stochastic repeats on fixed cohorts; the new model and protocol differ from the archived preliminary experiments. All 250 planned runs are retained; the accompanying report includes a sensitivity analysis excluding eight runs that required replacement contexts after technical interruptions.
